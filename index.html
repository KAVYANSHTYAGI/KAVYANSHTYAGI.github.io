About me

I'm the kind of person who reads a paper on optimal transport at midnight and then can't sleep because there's a cleaner formulation spinning around in their head. Calculus isn't just a tool for me — it's a language. Everything in my research, from Mamba state transitions to regret-based routing controllers, traces back to some beautiful piece of analysis.

01

Sapienza University of Rome · May 2026 – Present · with Paulo Russo

Physically Grounded State-Space Models for Hyperspectral Super-Resolution

Proposed AbSpec-Mamba and VolMamba v2 — models that condition selective state transitions on per-pixel material abundance, using prototype-guided reconstruction to recover fine spectral detail without hallucinating physically implausible spectral signatures.

Building content-adaptive multidimensional scanning and progressive coarse-to-fine reconstruction so the model restores high-resolution hyperspectral images even under severe sensor degradation and spectral corruption. This work directly extends the physical grounding missing from all prior SSM-based HSI methods.

Active
02

University of Tübingen · Sep 2025 – Present · with Andreas Zeigler

CE-GNN: Causally Constrained Graph Networks for Event-Based Vision

Formally characterized how four leading event-vision GNNs — AEGNN, SlideGCN, Voxel-GNN, HUGNet2+PA — leak future information backward in time, fundamentally violating the causality required for true real-time inference on event-camera streams.

Designing CE-GNN to enforce strict causal constraints via O(k log N) incremental graph construction, directed temporal attention, and predictive edge insertion. Keeps latency low while fully respecting event ordering. Manuscript in preparation.

Manuscript prep
03

IIT Ropar · May – Aug 2025 · with Dr. Puneet Goyal

SCAF: Robust Hyperspectral Classification via Optimal Transport

Identified a foundational flaw in standard HSI pipelines: treating spectral bands as independent inputs discards recoverable inter-band correlation. Recovered this signal using differentiable optimal-transport grouping with balanced Sinkhorn assignments that learn which bands naturally belong together.

Designed per-group Mamba processors that isolate sensor noise to individual groups, converting spectral redundancy from a liability into a graceful-degradation mechanism — achieving +11.6% accuracy under 20–30% band dropout against Transformer baselines.

Under review

3D Medical Segmentation & AI-Driven Malware Analysis

Reflara

A replay-native execution-intelligence platform for autonomous AI agents. Most multi-agent systems are black boxes — once something goes wrong, you can't go back. Reflara fixes that. It deterministically records and re-runs agent trajectories, reconstructs orchestration state, retrieval influence, and memory evolution over time — so you can reproduce, debug, and regression-test nondeterministic agent behavior step by step.

Publications & preprints

LightMedSeg: Lightweight 3D Medical Image Segmentation with Learned Spatial Anchors

Tyagi et al. · CVPR Workshops, Efficient Computer Vision (ECV 2026)

84.8% Dice on BraTS · 93.5% on ACDC · 0.48M parameters · 14 GFLOPs. Parameter-efficient 3D segmentation that makes volumetric medical imaging viable on memory-constrained clinical hardware, with strong label efficiency (78.4% Dice with 10% labeled data).

CVPR Workshop 2026

SCAF: Robust Hyperspectral Classification via Optimal Transport-Based Spectral Grouping

Tyagi et al. · Preprint, under review

Replaces independent band processing with differentiable optimal-transport grouping (balanced Sinkhorn). Mamba backbone cuts complexity from quadratic to linear. +11.6% accuracy under 20–30% band dropout. State-of-the-art clean performance across three benchmarks.

Under review
